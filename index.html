Servings below show how many it would take if you ate only that one food. In real life, mix a few foods across the day.

Good to know. Dry foods like dal and soya chunks soak up water when cooked, so their protein per 100 g falls a lot after cooking. That is why dry weights look so high. Compare each food using its serving size too.

About the numbers. Values are rounded and based on commonly used USDA-style food data. Real foods vary by type, brand and how you cook them.

Please note. Protein needs and targets vary from person to person. If you have a health condition, talk to a doctor or a dietitian before changing your diet.
